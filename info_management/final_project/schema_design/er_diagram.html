Federal Grant Disruption Database — Entity-Relationship Diagram

EPPS 6354 Information Management · Spring 2026 · Emily Stern · 6-table schema
Research question: How much federal grant funding has been disrupted since the 2025 administration change — either rescinded at the opportunity level (Grants.gov listing vanished) or terminated at the award level (per the five agency feeds) — and which agencies and recipients are most affected?
How to read it. Each rectangle is a table; the column above the line is the table name, the columns below are its fields. Italic fields with (PK) are primary keys, (FK) are foreign keys, and (UK) is a unique constraint. Diamond shapes show what the relationship between two tables means in plain English. The dashed self-arrows on agencies and recipients mean those tables can reference themselves — a row can have a parent row in the same table (for example, NIH's parent is HHS; a campus's parent is the system office).
