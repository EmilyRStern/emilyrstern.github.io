RESEARCH QUESTION

How much federal grant funding has been disrupted since the 2025 administration change — either rescinded at the opportunity level or terminated at the award level — and which agencies and recipients are most affected?

Every table in the schema that follows earns its place against this question.

What the question requires the schema to support

The research question breaks into six sub-questions. Each one points at the table(s) that must exist.

"Which Grants.gov opportunities disappeared?"
Need: opportunities + change_log
"Did those opportunities ever get awarded?"
Need: opportunities joined to awards
"Which awards were terminated?"
Need: awards joined to terminations
"Which agencies are most affected?"
Need: agencies with parent rollup
"Which recipients are most affected?"
Need: recipients with parent rollup
"How is disruption changing over time?"
Need: change_log + terminations on a shared timeline

Six sub-questions → six tables. Anything else is bloat.

The headline query

"Which agencies have lost the most funding, counting both rescissions and terminations?"

-- Disruption by agency: rescinded + terminated funding, with HHS sub-agencies
-- (NIH, CDC, SAMHSA) rolled up to their parent department.

SELECT
  COALESCE(parent.agency_name, child.agency_name) AS agency,
  COUNT(DISTINCT cl.opportunity_id)
       FILTER (WHERE cl.change_type = 'REMOVED')                AS rescinded_count,
  COUNT(DISTINCT tm.termination_id)
       FILTER (WHERE tm.has_been_terminated)                    AS terminated_count,
  SUM(CASE WHEN cl.change_type = 'REMOVED'
           THEN o.estimated_total_funding END)                  AS rescinded_dollars,
  SUM(CASE WHEN tm.has_been_terminated
           THEN tm.award_value END)                             AS terminated_dollars
FROM   agencies child
LEFT   JOIN agencies     parent ON parent.agency_id    = child.parent_agency_id
LEFT   JOIN opportunities o     ON o.agency_id         = child.agency_id
LEFT   JOIN change_log    cl    ON cl.opportunity_id   = o.opportunity_id
LEFT   JOIN terminations  tm    ON tm.source_agency_id = child.agency_id
GROUP  BY agency
ORDER  BY rescinded_dollars + terminated_dollars DESC;

Six full queries in sample_queries.sql — total disruption, by agency, by recipient, confirmed rescissions, monthly volatility, reinstatement rates.

Loading the data

  1. Reference layer — load agencies (departments first, then sub-agencies and offices). One-time seed.
  2. Recipients — deduplicate USA Spending CSVs by UEI; second pass joins parent UEIs.
  3. Awards — one row per award, with foreign keys to recipients and agencies.
  4. Opportunities + change_log — continue the existing daily Grants.gov pull; new changes append to change_log.
  5. Terminations — load each of the five agency CSVs into the unified shape.

Existing R scripts already do steps 4 and most of 2–3. Steps 1 and 5 are the new work.
